Song Contribution Program

WePlay welcomes talented musicians, independent artists, and music enthusiasts to contribute original audio tracks, karaoke instrumental accompaniments, and cover songs to our global voice room music library.

Creator Rewards & Licensing

Approved contributors receive exclusive creator badge titles, customized voice room microphone effects, monthly Gold Coin bounties, and potential revenue share based on track usage across party rooms.

Submission Guidelines

Submit high-quality audio files (MP3/WAV, min 320kbps) along with track metadata (Title, Artist, Lyrics, Proof of License) to support@wejoysg.com with the subject line "Song Contribution - [Artist Name]".
