WePlay Minor Protection Program

Mandatory age verification

Registration requires the user to fill in their birthdate, and there are strict restrictions on changing the rules to ensure the information is accurate. This, combined with multiple system protection mechanisms, safeguards the online environment for minors.

Real-name Authentication & Anti-Addiction System

WePlay enforces strict time limits and spending caps for underage players in compliance with global minor protection guidelines. Nighttime gaming restrictions are automatically enforced to promote healthy digital habits.

24/7 Content Moderation & Parental Controls

AI-powered text and voice filtering prevents toxic behavior, cyberbullying, and inappropriate content sharing. Parents can request account restrictions or monitoring assistance by reaching out to support@wejoysg.com.
